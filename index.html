The challenge

The organization needed one maintainable platform for a public website and corporate email, with different access paths for internal and external users, controlled outbound mail delivery, encrypted connectivity between locations, and repeatable recovery procedures — without one layer's failure taking down another.

What I built

  • A Linux hosting environment with HestiaCP, Nginx, Exim, Dovecot, and Roundcube.
  • A WireGuard tunnel from an external VPS into the internal MikroTik edge router.
  • HAProxy for controlled external access, and policy-based routing at the edge.
  • Domain-based outbound SMTP routing with direct and relayed delivery paths.

Architecture

How traffic actually moves

Deliberately generalized — no production identifiers, exact routes, or provider details.

01

External entry

External users and public DNS reach an external VPS running HAProxy and the SMTP relay path.

02

Encrypted tunnel

HAProxy forwards selected services across an encrypted WireGuard tunnel toward the internal edge.

03

MikroTik edge router

Internal users and tunnel traffic meet at the MikroTik router, governed by firewall, NAT, and routing policy.

04

Reverse proxy and hosting

Traffic reaches a reverse proxy and the Linux web/mail server hosting the site, Exim, Dovecot, and Roundcube.

05

Backup and monitoring

The hosting server also feeds scheduled backups and the logging/monitoring that drives recovery decisions.

Mail routing

Domain-based SMTP delivery

Direct delivery

Most hosted domains send outbound mail directly, authenticated by SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.

Relayed delivery

Domains that need it use a secure external relay transport instead — chosen per sender domain, not applied globally.

Incident recovery

A real production incident

The hosting control panel login returned HTTP 500 after required PHP vendor dependencies became unavailable following a filesystem-related change. Recovery separated hosted website content from control-panel application files, restored the dependency layer, verified service health, and documented preventive controls.
